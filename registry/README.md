# Registries

- `releases.json` — canonical legal release/candidate record and current digest.
- `candidates.json` — published pre-freeze candidate lineage by exact digest.
- `projects.json` — projects owned/controlled by the steward using a Kiyoshima profile.
- `adopters.json` — optional public third-party adoption records.
- `grants.public.json` — **only** grants intentionally safe to disclose publicly.

Private grants, commercial agreements, private identities, confidential consideration, and enforcement evidence must not be committed here merely for recordkeeping. A public grant record may contain a detached document digest and minimal non-sensitive identity/scope fields when disclosure is intentional.
