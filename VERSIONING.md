# Versioning

The framework version and legal-license version are distinct.

- `Kiyoshima Source License 1.0` is the legal version.
- `Kiyoshima Licensing Framework 1.0.0` is the surrounding tooling/metadata release.

Legal text uses deliberate semantic versioning:

- editorial changes that can affect matching or interpretation still require a new published license version;
- no released legal text is modified in place;
- projects remain on the exact version they declared unless the copyright holder affirmatively relicenses them.

Recommended Git tags:

- `source-v1.0` for the frozen legal text;
- `framework-v1.0.0` for the complete framework release.
