# FAQ

## Is Kiyoshima Open a new Open Source license?

No. Kiyoshima Open is a framework/profile declaration. The controlling legal
license remains the standard SPDX expression, for example `MIT`, `Apache-2.0`,
or `MIT OR Apache-2.0`.

## Why have an Open profile if it is not a new license?

It lets projects use one Kiyoshima Passport/tooling workflow across both
unrestricted OSS and Kiyoshima Source products without forcing an unknown legal
license onto open projects.

## Is Kiyoshima Source 1.0 open source?

No. It is source-available. It intentionally restricts organization production
and commercial exploitation, so it does not satisfy the Open Source Definition.

## Can a freelancer use Kiyoshima Source for paid work?

Yes, as a tool while acting as an Individual and within the Source terms.
Selling/transferring the Covered Software itself, embedding it into a client
deliverable, or operating it as the paid product/service requires separate
permission.

## Can a company evaluate Source before paying?

Yes. The current candidate includes a 30-day internal, isolated, non-production
Organization Evaluation Use grant. Production or ordinary operational use still
requires an agreement.

## Can a company use a Kiyoshima Open project in production?

Kiyoshima adds no organization-use restriction. The controlling standard
license and its conditions apply.

## Can an AI coding agent edit a Source project?

Yes when acting on behalf of a principal who already has the underlying right.
The agent does not create new permissions.

## Does Open automatically mean AI model training is legally cleared?

No. The Open Passport records that Kiyoshima adds no AI-specific field-of-use
restriction. The controlling standard license, applicable law, third-party
material, and the concrete training workflow still matter. Preflight reports
this as conditional rather than unconditional authorization.

## Does KIYOSHIMA.json grant rights?

No. It is informational machine-readable metadata. The legal instrument controls.

## Can another developer use Kiyoshima Source 1.0?

The candidate text permits another developer to apply an exact, unmodified copy
to software they have authority to license. Modified texts must not be presented
as the official Kiyoshima Source license.

## Can Kiyoshima relicense dependencies?

No. A project author can only license material they control or are authorized to
license. Third-party components retain their own license terms.
