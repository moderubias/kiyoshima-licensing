## Licensing

This project uses the **Kiyoshima Open** profile.

Controlling legal license expression: **`REPLACE_WITH_SPDX_EXPRESSION`**.

Kiyoshima Open is not a separate software license and does not modify the
standard license terms. `KIYOSHIMA.json` provides machine-readable licensing
metadata for tooling, CI, IDEs, agents, and policy engines; the legal license
text remains controlling.

See `LICENSE` and `KIYOSHIMA.json`. Compound expressions may also keep their complete component texts in `LICENSES/`.
