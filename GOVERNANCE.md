# Governance

## Stewardship

Kiyoshima Licensing Framework has a designated license steward. Stewardship may be assigned to a successor entity without retroactively changing an already published license version.

## Immutable released versions

Once a legal version is declared final and tagged, its canonical text is immutable. Typographical, legal, policy, or semantic changes require a new version.

## Machine metadata

Schemas and explanatory metadata may evolve independently when they do not change legal rights. A machine-schema change that would materially alter permission semantics must receive a new schema version.

## Conservative automation rule

No machine-readable file can silently expand a legal grant. An automation encountering malformed, absent, ambiguous, conflicting, or unknown values must treat the relevant right as not established and request human or authenticated permission.

## Public change process

Proposed legal changes should be discussed in public issues or pull requests before release. Release notes must identify legal changes, migration impact, and whether an older license remains supported.
